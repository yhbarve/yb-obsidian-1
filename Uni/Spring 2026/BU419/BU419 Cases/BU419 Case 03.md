Q1.
- Internal Risks
	- Problems with internal control and corporate governance.
- External Corporate Risks
	- Financial risks from the parent company, other subsidiaries, reinsurers, other related parties (e.g. customers, suppliers, regulators)
	- Such risks could cause contagion effect and cripple a well-run insurance company.
- Systemic Risks
	- Pandemics, regulatory negligence, global economic downturns, natural disasters
