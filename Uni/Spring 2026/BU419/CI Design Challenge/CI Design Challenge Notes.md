### Program Objectives
- **Primary Goal — Affordability & Availability:** Keeps high-risk zones (e.g., BC) affordable through a national baseline pool while ensuring private insurers stay willing to write policies.
- **Secondary Goals — Resilience & Mitigation:** Encourages proactive seismic retrofitting through explicit discounts while reducing the national protection gap.
### 1. Coverage Scope and Participation
- **Peril Scope:** Single-peril program dedicated to earthquake, with explicit coverage included for "fire-following" losses to avoid claims disputes.
- **Coverage Structure:** Sold as an endorsement to standard homeowners policies.
- **Eligible Properties:** Residential properties initially (homeowners and condo units).
- **Participation:**
    - **Insurers:** Mandatory to offer earthquake coverage on all property policies.
    - **Policyholders:** Mandatory purchase required by mortgage lenders in high-risk seismic zones; optional elsewhere.
### 2. Pricing and Cross-Subsidization
- **Property-Level Pricing Model:** Hybrid pricing model. Uses a flat national baseline fee combined with risk-based "sliders" (location, soil type, build year).
- **Cross-Subsidization Structure:** Low-risk regions partially subsidize high-risk areas via the flat baseline fee (national solidarity).
- **Moral Hazard Management:** Mitigated by offering 10–15% premium discounts for verified risk mitigation (e.g., foundation bolting, seismic bracing).
- **Regulatory Capital Requirements:** Public backing lowers the regulatory capital burden on private insurers, making premiums cheaper.
### 3. Program Capitalization and Funding
- **Initial Capitalization:** Seeded via a combination of government disaster funds and mandatory initial insurer capital contributions.
- **Ongoing Funding:** Pre-funded reserve pool accumulated through policyholder premiums, backed by post-event government liquidity support if reserves are depleted by a catastrophic event.
### 4. Pooling and Governance
- **Loss Pooling:** Primary insurers pool earthquake risk into a central national catastrophe pool.
- **Governance Framework:** Nationally consistent framework to ensure equal consumer protection, rules, and pricing structure across all provinces.
### 5. Government Role in the Catastrophe Risk Stack
- **Risk Stack Position:**
    - **Primary Layer:** Private insurers retain the initial deductible/small losses.
    - **Middle Layer:** Central pool absorbs intermediate losses using pre-funded reserves and commercial reinsurance.
    - **Top Layer:** Government acts as an excess-of-loss backstop for extreme tail risk (e.g., 1-in-500 year Megathrust event).
### 6. Operational Roles
- **Pricing:** Set by a public pool entity using standardized risk models.
- **Underwriting & Servicing:** Administered entirely by private primary insurers via existing customer channels.
- **Claims Management:** Private insurers handle all claims on the front end to avoid customer confusion (single point of contact), reimbursed by the central pool for covered pool losses.
### Summary of Government Roles
- Acts primarily as a **Regulator** (mandating offers/coverage rules), **Reinsurer/Backstop** (taking top-layer tail risk), and **Liquidity Provider** (guaranteeing claims-paying ability post-event).
