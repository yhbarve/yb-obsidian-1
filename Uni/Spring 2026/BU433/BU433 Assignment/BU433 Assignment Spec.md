## Guidelines:
The assignment warrants an in-depth performance and risk analysis of an actual financial institution. The selected financial institution can be an U.S. or Canadian firm, or a global entity with substantial presence in North American financial markets. The chosen financial institution can belong to any one of the five categories discussed in class: depository institutions (chartered banks and credit unions), non-bank finance companies (loan agencies), securities and brokerage firms and investment banks, investment funds (mutual funds, hedge funds, pension funds, and other funds) and insurance companies (life insurance and property and casualty insurance). The financial institution needs to be currently existing and have at least twenty years of history.
Emphasis lies on creative and original thinking, skillful analysis, and professional execution. A regurgitation or compilation of published materials or online articles is not permitted; lack of originality will be penalized. In preparing your report, take the viewpoint of: (1) as a financial manager or a risk analyst in the financial institution, or (2) as a consultant to the financial institution.
