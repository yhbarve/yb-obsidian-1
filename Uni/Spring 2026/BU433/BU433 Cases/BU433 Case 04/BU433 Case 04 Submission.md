### ID: 211544920

**Q1.**
- **Asset-liability mismatch:** During the tech boom of 2020–2021, SVB's deposits surged from $62 billion to $189 billion.
- **The Fed's Rate Hikes:** When the Federal Reserve rapidly hiked interest rates to combat inflation, the market value of SVB’s long-term bonds fell massively.
- **Sector Concentration:** SVB’s depositors were heavily concentrated in venture capital-backed startups. When venture funding dried up in late 2022, these startups began steadily withdrawing their deposits to fund operations.
- **Twitter-Fueled Bank Run:** Worried about losses, prominent VCs instructed their portfolio companies to pull their funds. Depositors withdrew $42 billion in a single day using online banking. This forced regulators to step in and shut down the insolvent bank.
- **Risk Management Failures:** SVB operated without a Chief Risk Officer for nine months and repeatedly ignored its own internal interest-rate risk limits and warnings.
