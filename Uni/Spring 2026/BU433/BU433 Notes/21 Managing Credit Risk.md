# Credit Risk Management
- Financial institutions are special because of their ability to efficiently transform financial claims of household savers into claims issued to corporations, individuals, and governments.
- This ability to process and evaluate information and control and monitor borrowers allows them to transform these claims at the lowest possible cost to all parties.
- Credit risk management is important for FI managers because it involves the determination of several features of a loan or debt instrument.
- Credit quality of many FIs' lending and investment decisions has attracted a great deal of attention over the past three decades.
- Managerial efficiency and credit risk management strategies directly affect the return and risks of the loan portfolio.
- One advantage that FIs have over individual investors is the ability to diversify some credit risk by exploiting the law of large numbers.
- A credit quality problem can cause an FI to become insolvent in the worst case, or it can result in such a significant drain on earnings and net worth that it can adversely affect the FI's profitability and ability to compete.

# Credit Analysis: Real Estate Lending
- Residential mortgage loan applications are among the most standardized of all credit applications.
- 2 considerations dominate an FI's decision to approve a mortgage loan application:
	1. Applicant's ability and willingness to make timely interest and principal repayments
	2. Value of Borrower's Collateral
- GDS and TDS are two ratios useful in determining a customer's ability to maintain mortgage payments.
## Debt Service Ratio (GDS)
- This is equal to the total accommodation expenses divided by gross income.
- Acceptable threshold is generally set around a maximum of 25% to 30%.
## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDS)
- This is equal to the total accommodation expenses plus all other debt service payments divided by gross income.
- Acceptable threshold generally is set around a maximum of 35% to 40%.
