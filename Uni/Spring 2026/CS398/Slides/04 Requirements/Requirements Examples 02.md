### Guideline 5: Prompt the Model to Detect Conflicts and Undefined Terms Across the Full Requirements Set

**Example:**

> **Problem:** Your team has assembled the following five requirements for an e-commerce platform from three separate elicitation sessions. Before handing them to developers, you need to check for contradictions and for any shared terms that lack a clear definition. Use the model to find them.
>
> - REQ-01: Users must authenticate before accessing any feature.
> - REQ-02: Guest users may browse the product catalog without login.
> - REQ-03: A user may delete their account at any time without delay.
> - REQ-04: Search results shall load within 2 seconds.
> - REQ-09: All user data must be retained for 90 days for regulatory compliance.
